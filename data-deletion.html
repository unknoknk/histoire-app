User data deletion

Histoire stores no user data on any server. The only data it keeps are sign-in tokens, saved in local files on the owner's computer.

How to delete your data

  1. Revoke the app's access on each platform:
    • Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites, then remove Histoire.
    • Facebook: Settings and privacy, then Apps and websites, then remove Histoire.
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps and services, then remove Histoire.
    • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com, then Security, then Third-party apps, then remove Histoire.
  2. Delete the local token files (the secrets folder of the app).
