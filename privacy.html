Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Data we access

When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), through the user.info.basic scope. It uses these only to show which account is connected and to upload videos that you choose, through the video.upload and video.publish scopes.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps and services). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
