Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Histoire is a personal desktop tool. Its owner uses it to publish their own videos to their own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

Data we access

When you sign in with a platform (TikTok Login Kit, Instagram Login or Google sign-in), the app receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID, username or display name, profile picture). It uses them only to show which account is connected and to publish the videos you choose. On TikTok this uses the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish scopes. On Instagram it uses instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_content_publish. On YouTube it uses the youtube.upload scope.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in local files. No data is sent to any server other than the platform's own servers. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deletion

See User data deletion.
